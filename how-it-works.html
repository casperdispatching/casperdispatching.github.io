How It Works

Home / How It Works

  1. Tell us about your truck

    Equipment, preferred lanes and radius, home time, and your minimum rate per mile.

  2. We find the loads

    We search load boards and talk to brokers to find freight that fits.

  3. You approve in writing

    Say yes or no to each load. No forced dispatch.

  4. Run it, get paid, then pay us

    Our fee is due only after you've been paid.

Ready to get loaded?

Email us your equipment and preferred lanes and we'll take it from there.
