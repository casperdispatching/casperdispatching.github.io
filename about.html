Who we are

Your dispatcher, not a broker

Casper Dispatching is a trucking dispatch company based in Brownsville, Texas, serving carriers across the Rio Grande Valley and beyond.

We work for you: every load runs under your own MC, you decide which loads you take, and we get paid only after you do.

Send us your equipment and lanes. We'll take it from there.
